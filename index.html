Privacy Policy

Expert IT Support built the Prophet Bananana app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by Expert IT Support at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

Prophet Bananana does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from users. All fortune content is loaded from local assets bundled within the app. No internet connection is required for core functionality.

The app does not:

  • Collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Access device contacts, calendar, or photos
  • Use location services
  • Share data with third parties

PDF Export Feature

When you use the PDF export feature, the generated PDF file is saved locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or access these PDF files.

Video Player

The app includes a bundled meditation video played locally. No video streaming or external video service is used.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
